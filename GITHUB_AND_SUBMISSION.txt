Project title:

Short project summary:
QueuePact routes community-hall requests to the venue or programme team using
GenLayer AI consensus. Mixed or unclear requests stay unassigned for review,
while excluded work is marked out of scope. Only the assigned team wallet can
acknowledge a queued ticket, with the original decision and ticket hash preserved.

Why GenLayer:
Requests arrive in ordinary language. Validators independently evaluate the full
request against a fixed policy, compare the route and audit its explanation.
Deterministic contract code then binds acknowledgement permission to the assigned
team wallet. A shared decision changes who can act on the ticket.

What makes it distinct:
This project manages routing and acknowledgement of task responsibility. It does
not manage stock holds, bilingual notices, source origins, experimental holdouts,
brief-change consent or explanation-gated progress from the earlier projects.
